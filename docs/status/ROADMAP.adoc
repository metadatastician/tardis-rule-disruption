== Intent (recorded 2026-09-02, owner ruling)

TARDIS is the full thing, a super step ahead of the rest of the line: find a way to make a Conway Game of Life set-up look as much like "reality" as it can possibly be, with the absolute requirement of never breaking the laws. The acronym leads with *Totally* on purpose: Totally-Adherent Rule-Disruption Imitation System. It is about *appearing* to break the rules even though that is not what happens. Two parts are recorded separately. The hard invariant is that B3/S23 is never broken, which is checkable. The open target is that the result resembles reality, which stays unmeasured until a measure is chosen. It is a lifetime, open-ended research project, probably never to be completed and quite possibly impossible; the hope is that after all the mathematics games and demonstrations along the way, people will think that maybe anything is possible, or close to it, and take it off in other directions with weird applications. Even if the reality target is out of reach, it is adaptable as a maths challenge, an educational piece on constraints, or game-design thinking. The owner's own tagline, offered as a joke: "maybe I'm the next Euclid... Euclid with cells rather than the floorboard tiles."
